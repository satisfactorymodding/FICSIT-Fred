# FICSIT-Fred — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 16, 2026

## What we collect

To track message XP, FICSIT-Fred stores the following in a database:

- Your Discord **username**
- Your Discord **user ID**
- Your **XP / level data** (message counts and related totals)

## What we don't collect

- Message content is not stored. Messages are only counted for XP.
- No emails, IP addresses, or other personal information.
- No analytics or tracking tools, and no data sold or shared with third parties.

## How we use it

This data is used only to calculate and display XP and levels. It is not used for any other purpose.

## Storage and retention

Data is kept in a database operated by the developer [hosted on PROVIDER, if applicable]. It is kept for as long as the bot is active in a server you're part of, or until you ask us to delete it.

## Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us below. We will process requests within a reasonable time.

## Discord

Discord processes your data under its own [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy). This policy covers only FICSIT-Fred.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above will reflect any changes.
